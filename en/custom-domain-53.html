Custom domain

Publish your Zyllio application using your own domain name, such as www.my-app.com.

Available with the Professional plan.

This integration lets you associate a domain name with your Zyllio application, then activate it with an SSL certificate.

Enable the custom domain

In Zyllio, open your application settings, then the Custom domain section.

Enable the integration using the toggle at the top of the page.

1. Enter your domain

In the Domain name field, enter the address you want to use, for example www.my-app.com.

You can enter the domain with or without https://. Zyllio automatically removes the protocol and any path so that only the domain name is retained.

The domain must be valid and include at least one subdomain and a top-level domain. Make sure you have purchased it and can modify its DNS records through your domain provider, such as OVH, Gandi, or Namecheap.

2. Generate the DNS configuration

Click Configure. Zyllio immediately generates the DNS settings required for your domain.

The page displays a single DNS record with its type, host, and value. Depending on your domain, this is either an A or CNAME record.

3. Add the DNS record

Sign in to your domain provider's administration interface, then open its DNS record management page.

Add the record displayed by Zyllio, using its exact type, host, and value. Do not replace these values with a generic configuration.

4. Check DNS propagation

After saving the change with your provider, return to Zyllio and click Check propagation.

DNS propagation can take from 5 minutes to 24 hours. If the check fails, wait a few minutes and try again. Also check that the type, host, and value of the record exactly match those displayed in Zyllio.

5. Activate the secure domain

When the check succeeds, click Activate secure domain.

Zyllio then generates the SSL certificate and deploys your application on your domain over HTTPS. Once activation is complete, your users can access the application at the configured address.
